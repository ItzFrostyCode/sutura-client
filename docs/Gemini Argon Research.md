> B. Technical & Operational Breakdown of 1st District Production Hubs
>
> 1. School Uniforms & Clinical/Medical Scrub Suits (San Pedro St., Ilustre St., & Matina Crossing)
>
> * Primary Geography: Centered along San Pedro St. (Brgy. 2-A & 3-A), Ilustre St. (Brgy. 3-A & 4-A), and McArthur Highway in Matina Crossing (Brgy. 74-A).
> * Institutional Client Base:
>   * Medical & Allied Health Schools/Hospitals: San Pedro College (SPC), Davao Doctors College (DDC), Brokenshire College, UIC, DMSF, and Southern Philippines Medical Center (SPMC) personnel.
>   * General Universities & K-12: Ateneo de Davao University (Claveria & Matina campuses), University of Mindanao (Bolton & Matina campuses), Holy Cross of Davao College (HCDC), STI College Davao, and DepEd teachers.
> * Standard Fabric Specifications Carried by Davao Tailors:
>   * Clinical Scrubs & Lab Coats: Peach Twill (standard durable scrub fabric), Lacoste Cotton/Poly (breathable piqué weave), and 4-Way Stretch Spandex / Katrina (modern jogger-cut scrubs).
>   * School Blouses, Polos & Skirts: Tetoron Cotton, Katrina, * Gabardine*, and arman/ Hugo Boss suiting for trousers and blazers.
> * Ordering & Fitting Workflow:
>   1. Individual Walk-In (Made-to-Measure): Client visits shop -> Master cutter takes 8–12 body measurements -> Client pays 50% downpayment -> Pattern drafting & sewing (typically 5–10 working days during off-peak; 2–3 weeks during the June–August back-to-school rush) -> Final fitting & balance payment.
>   2. Ready-Made + Instant Alteration: Many San Pedro St. shops pre-sew standard sizes (XS to 3XL) of SPC/DDC/UIC/UM uniforms and clinical scrubs, requiring only 1–2 days to attach specific year-level patches or hem trousers.
>
> 2. Full-Sublimation Sportswear, Organization Jerseys, & Corporate Polos (Obrero, Bonifacio St., Magsaysay Ave., & Ma-a)
>
> * Primary Geography: Concentrated in Bonifacio St. / Artiaga St. (Brgy. 33-D to 36-D), Bo. Obrero (Brgy. 14-B to 20-B), Trading Blvd. (Brgy. 37-D), and Brgy. Ma-a (Talomo).
> * Two-Tier Ecosystem in Davao City:
>   * Tier A — Large Industrial Print & Subcontracting Hubs (e.g., Paintline Digital & Textile Printing Corp. on Bonifacio St., IBYK Printing Services, Aranes Bldg. 8-Color Hub in Ma-a): Operate high-speed industrial sublimation printers (such as Asparas G5 or 8-color hybrid printers) and large rotary calender heat presses. They offer two service tracks:  Facebook  
>     * Category A (Print & Press Only / Subcontracting): Smaller neighborhood tailors from Toril, Calinan, Buhangin, or Agdao email their digital pattern layouts; the hub prints on transfer paper and heat-presses onto fabric rolls, while the neighborhood tailor handles cutting and sewing.  Facebook  
>     * Category B (Full Package Production): End-to-end layout, printing, heat transfer, laser/manual cutting, and overlock/coverstitch sewing for direct team/corporate clients.  Facebook  
>   * Tier B — Boutique Sportswear & Cut-and-Sew Shops (e.g., WePrint PH Davao, Hendrix Tailoring & Sublimation): Focus on custom graphic design (free layout typically starting at 10–12 pcs MOQ with up to 2 revisions), sizing charts (Kids to 5XL), and roster management (individual player surnames, numbers, and sizes).  Facebook  
> * Standard Sublimation Fabrics in Davao:
>   * Microfiber Drifit / Eyelet Drifit (standard breathable mesh for basketball/volleyball jerseys and event shirts).
>   * Polydex / Heavy Spandex (preferred for corporate polo shirts, hoodies, and cycling/motocross long-sleeves).
>   * Aircool / Honeycomb (lightweight running singlets and pickleball/badminton shirts).
> * Standard Production Workflow & Turnaround:
>   1. Digital Mockup & Roster Lock (Days 1–2): Client submits logos, color palette (e.g., Navy, Royal Blue, Gold, White), collar style (V-neck, round neck, Chinese collar w/ zipper, or traditional polo collar), and spreadsheet of player/member names, numbers, and sizes.  Facebook  
>   2. Test Swatch / Color Proofing (Day 3): High-tier shops offer sample swatch printing onto actual drifit fabric to verify color accuracy after heat pressing (since sublimation inks shift saturation under 200°C heat).  Facebook  
>   3. RIP Sizing, Printing & Calender Heat Press (Days 4–6): Graphic artist nests individual pattern pieces (S, M, L, XL, 2XL) in CorelDRAW/Illustrator, prints onto sublimation transfer paper, and transfers ink into white polyester fabric.
>   4. Cut, Sew, & Quality Control (Days 7–10): Panels are cut and assembled using 4-thread overlock and flatbed interlock machines; collars, ribbing, or buttons are attached. Standard turnaround is 7–14 working days from layout approval and 50% downpayment.

     * *Mode A (Add-on Capability to Garment Production):* Uniform and barong tailors ( *San Pedro/Ilustre cluster* ,  *Cleopatra Tailoring* , couture ateliers) include school patches, embroidered logos, nameplates, or barong callado/embroidery as part of making a uniform or formal garment.

   * Custom formalwear and made-to-measure garments never publish static flat prices; they require measurement and design consultation (quotation workflow).
