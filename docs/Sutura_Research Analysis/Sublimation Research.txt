Sublimation allows for an exceptional level of detail, making it ideal for creating intricate designs on clothing, especially sports clothing and other performance-oriented apparel. What is Sublimation Printing?
At Fabrica MNL Inc., we use sublimation printing to bring your team's or brand's designs to life in vivid, full color. This process involves printing your designs onto a special transfer sheet, which is then applied to the fabric using high heat and pressure. The heat turns the ink into gas, allowing it to fuse directly into the fibers of the fabric—rather than sitting on top like traditional prints.

The result? A lightweight, vibrant print that feels like part of the garment itself. It won’t crack, fade, or peel over time, even with repeated washing. Sublimation is ideal for all-over designs and is best suited for polyester-based materials, making it the top choice for sportswear, uniforms, and performance apparel.
Why Sublimation Printing?
Sublimation printing is the go-to method when you want full color, full body prints that won’t fade, crack, or peel. It’s specifically designed for use on 100% white polyester fabrics, making it perfect for custom sportswears, activewear, personalized shirts, vests, and even printed socks. Because the design becomes part of the fabric, you get a finish that’s incredibly soft, breathable, and long-lasting.
