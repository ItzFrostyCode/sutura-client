# SUTURA FINAL SYSTEM SYNTHESIS

## PHASE 1 — BUSINESS MODEL COLLISION ANALYSIS

**A. Which business flows overlap?**
Both tailoring (Doc A) and fashion shops (Doc B) share a critical operational core: **Appointments (for fitting/measurements), Customer Records (the "Suki" system), Deposits (50% DP or Refundable Security), and Status Tracking (Cutting, Sewing, Ready for Pickup).** 

**B. Which flows exist in business reality but are weak in SUTURA?**
- **Deposit & Liability Tracking:** Real shops survive on 50% DPs and refundable rental deposits. SUTURA currently treats payments as a single flat record rather than separating "Earned Revenue" from "Refundable Liability."
- **Showcase & Proof Gallery:** Shops win clients by posting "Client Album" photos, not just catalog items. SUTURA lacks a portfolio-centric showcase.
- **Physical Store Dependency (The "Vibe" & Fit Risk):** Real businesses force offline conversions for fittings. SUTURA’s current e-commerce-like flow assumes online order finalization, which is unnatural for formalwear.

**C. Which flows exist in SUTURA but feel unnatural in real business?**
- **Complex Ready-to-Wear (RTW) E-commerce:** Treating RTW like Shopee checkout ignores the local "fake screenshot" scam culture and the necessity of physical inspection.
- **Inventory Expectations:** Real shops track "Dirty/Clean" or "Rack A" for rentals, not strict SKU bin locations. (Note: Thesis forbids inventory, so this must be abstracted).
- **Strict Role Separation on the Floor:** In reality, the Shop Owner is often the Tailor and the Cashier. Forcing users to switch accounts to update a status and log a payment causes friction.

**D. Which workflows are overcomplicated?**
- **Branch Switching vs. Order Routing:** Managing multiple branches as completely isolated databases is overcomplicated. In reality, the Main branch handles production; satellites handle intake. 
- **Settings Fragmentation:** Splitting "Shop Settings" and "Account Settings" into deeply separated tabs with branch management hidden inside account settings creates severe navigation fatigue.

**E. Which workflows are missing?**
- **Inquiry/Reservation State:** The gap between "Customer sends a message" and "Appointment is booked."
- **Rental Return & Inspection Flow:** Tracking the physical return of an item, inspection for damage, and deposit release.
- **Alteration/Rework Loop:** The ability to revert a "Ready for Pickup" status back to "Sewing/Adjustment" if the fitting fails.

### OUTPUT: CORE UNIFIED BUSINESS MODEL FOR SUTURA
SUTURA operates as a **Centralized Service Tracker & Discovery Platform**, not an e-commerce checkout. The unified model is:
**Discovery → Inquiry (Messenger/In-app) → Appointment Booking + Deposit Payment → Physical Fitting/Measurement → Job Order Creation (or Rental Reservation) → Production Tracking → Final Fitting → Balance Payment → Pickup/Return.**

## PHASE 3 — ROLE SYSTEM AUDIT (CLEAN RBAC ARCHITECTURE)

**1. System Admin (Platform Governance)**
- **Owns:** Subscription plans, Platform analytics, Verification status.
- **Triggers:** Approves/Rejects Shop registrations. Manages global categories.
- **Cannot Access:** Individual shop job orders, customer measurements, staff profiles.

**2. Shop Owner (Business Operator)**
- **Owns:** Shop profile, Branches, Catalog/Services, Staff accounts, Subscriptions.
- **Triggers:** Assigns Job Orders to staff. Approves appointments. Verifies payments. 
- **Cannot Access:** System-wide analytics, other shops' data.

**3. Staff (Workshop/Floor Executor)**
- **Owns:** Their assigned queue. 
- **Sees:** Customer profiles & measurements *only for orders assigned to them*.
- **Triggers:** Updates production status (Cutting → Sewing → Ready). Logs measurements. 
- **Cannot Access:** Billing/Subscription settings, Shop financial reports, other staff's queues.

**4. Customer (End-User)**
- **Owns:** Their profile, saved measurements, appointment history, order tracking.
- **Triggers:** Books appointments, sends inquiries, leaves reviews, uploads payment receipts.
- **Cannot Access:** Shop dashboard, staff details, other customer profiles.

## PHASE 4 — SHOP OWNER EXPERIENCE AUDIT (UX ARCHITECTURE)

**The Problem:** The current layout causes a "logout feeling"—jumping from a static CRUD Catalog page to a Calendar to a Payment queue feels like using three different disconnected software tools. 

**The Solution:** Transform the dashboard from a "Database Manager" into an "Operational Workspace" (Shopee Seller Center / Meta Business Suite model).

**Ideal Shop Owner Workspace Layout:**
- **Top Bar:** Global Search (Find any customer/order instantly) + Branch Context Switcher + Notifications (New inquiries, late payments) + Quick Actions (New Order, Log Payment).
- **Left Sidebar (Task-Oriented, not Object-Oriented):**
  - 🏠 **Home:** Actionable metrics. "X orders need QC," "Y appointments today," "Z unpaid balances."
  - 📅 **Schedule & Inbox:** Unified view of upcoming appointments and customer inquiries.
  - ✂️ **Production Queue:** The core Job Order pipeline (Kanban board: Pending → Cutting → Sewing → Fitting → Ready).
  - 💳 **Payments:** Simple ledger of "Deposits Collected" vs "Balances Due."
  - 🛍️ **Storefront:** The Catalog & Services manager. Where portfolio photos and rental items are posted.
  - 👥 **Suki CRM:** Customer database with measurement histories.
  - ⚙️ **Settings:** Shop profile, staff, branches, subscription.

*This eliminates the CRUD feeling. The owner logs in and immediately sees their "To-Do" list for the day, not a blank table of database rows.*

## PHASE 5 — ORDER PIPELINE NORMALIZATION

To avoid duplicated logic, unify all business models into **Listing Types** within a single Catalog, which then route to the appropriate pipeline.

**Unified Listing Types:**
1. **Made-to-Order (MTO) / Custom Tailoring** (Gowns, Barongs, Uniforms)
2. **Alteration / Repair** (Racing, adjusting)
3. **Rental** (Per piece or Event Package)
4. **Ready-Made / Used Item Sale** (Liquidation stock)
5. **Showcase Only** (Portfolio photos, no direct transaction)

**Pipeline Routing:**
- **MTO & Alteration:** Creates an **Appointment** (for measurement) → Converts to a **Job Order** (with production tracking) → Triggers **Payment** (50% DP, 50% Balance).
- **Rental:** Creates a **Catalog Order** → Triggers **Payment** (Rental Fee + Refundable Deposit) → Tracks Dates (Pickup Date → Return Date) → Does *not* enter production Job Order queue unless alteration is needed.
- **Used/Ready-Made Sale:** Creates a **Catalog Order** → Triggers **Payment** (Full amount) → Status: "Ready for Pickup".
- **Showcase Only:** Allows Customer to send an **Inquiry** (Appointment request) to replicate a design.

*By normalizing this, a single "Orders" module can display all types using a "Type" filter, rather than having separate databases for Job Orders and Catalog Orders.*

## FINAL OUTPUT REQUIRED

### 1. Final Unified SUTURA Business Model
SUTURA is a **Web-Based Tailoring & Fashion Service Tracker**. It bridges the gap between Facebook-based inquiries and physical workshop execution. It does not sell products like an e-commerce store; it **books services, tracks custom production, and manages rental lifecycles**. Customers discover shops via map/garment filters, inquire, and book physical appointments. Shop owners log 50% downpayments, take digital measurements, and update production stages. The system ensures transparency from the first fitting to the final garment pickup.

### 2. Final Clean Module Structure
- **Admin Portal:** Shop Approvals, Subscription Plans, Platform Analytics.
- **Shop Owner Dashboard:**
  - *Command Center:* Home (To-dos), Schedule & Appointments, Payments Ledger.
  - *Workroom:* Production Queue (Job Orders), Customer CRM (Sukis & Measurements).
  - *Storefront:* Catalog (Listings: MTO, Rental, Showcase, Sale), Reviews.
  - *Configuration:* Shop Profile & Branches, Staff Management, Subscription Billing.
- **Staff Dashboard:** My Production Queue, My Appointments, Customer Measurements.
- **Customer Portal:** Map Discovery, Shop Profiles, My Appointments, My Orders (Track Status), My Measurements.

### 3. Final Role Architecture
- **Admin:** Gatekeeper. Governs platform integrity and tenant subscriptions.
- **Shop Owner:** Business Manager. Configures storefront, manages team, oversees all operational tracking and finances.
- **Staff:** Executor. Interacts only with assigned daily tasks (measurements, status updates).
- **Customer:** Consumer. Searches, books, tracks, and reviews.

### 4. Final Order Flow Architecture
1. **Inquiry:** Customer finds Shop via Map/Catalog → Sends Inquiry / Booking Request.
2. **Intake:** Shop Owner approves Appointment → Customer pays Deposit (logged manually).
3. **Execution (Custom/MTO):** Staff takes Measurements → Creates Job Order → Updates stages (Cutting → Sewing).
4. **Execution (Rental):** Shop logs Catalog Order → Marks as "Reserved" → Awaits Pickup.
5. **Fitting/QA:** Custom garments enter "Fitting" stage → Adjustments loop if needed.
6. **Closure:** Customer pays Balance (logged) → Status: "Released/Picked Up". (If Rental: Status: "Returned" → Deposit refunded).

### 5. Final Shop Owner UX Architecture
**Fluid, Business-First, Action-Driven.**
- **No more "CRUD tables."** The default view is an operational dashboard highlighting *today's* bottlenecks (e.g., "3 garments awaiting QC", "2 unpaid balances").
- **Unified Search:** A single search bar to find "Juan dela Cruz" (Customer) or "JOB-1024" (Order) instantly.
- **Kanban Production Board:** Job orders are drag-and-drop cards moving across columns (Pending → Cutting → Sewing → Ready). This mimics physical garment tags moving through a workshop.
- **Quick-Action Floating Buttons:** "Log Payment," "New Appointment," "Upload Client Photo" accessible from any screen.
- **Landmark Manager:** A dedicated field in Shop Settings for "Nearest Landmark" that auto-appends to public profiles and map pins, solving the #1 PH customer pain point.

### 6. Final Thesis-Safe Version of SUTURA
The strongest capstone-aligned version of SUTURA strictly adheres to the approved documentation:
- It features a **Map-Based Discovery Module** utilizing Google Maps API for Davao City tailors.
- It implements a **Subscription Tier System** (Basic, Pro, Premium) gated by the Admin.
- It utilizes a **Tailoring Shop Dashboard** where owners manage staff, branches, and services.
- The core engine is the **Order Tracking and Measurement Module**, where staff version-control JSON body dimensions and update sequential production milestones.
- Payments are **tracked manually** (deposit/balance), respecting the exclusion of third-party gateways.
- It entirely **excludes inventory, payroll, and courier APIs**, ensuring 100% compliance with the approved scope limitations. 

This system is realistic, locally contextualized, and technologically bounded—making it the perfect IT capstone project.
